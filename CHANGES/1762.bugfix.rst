Fixed :meth:`~yarl.URL.build` accepting ports outside the valid TCP/UDP
range (0-65535) without error. Out-of-range ports now raise
``ValueError`` at construction time, matching the existing validation in
:meth:`~yarl.URL.with_port`. Previously an out-of-range port was silently
accepted by ``build`` and only raised during stringification, far from
the actual misuse.
